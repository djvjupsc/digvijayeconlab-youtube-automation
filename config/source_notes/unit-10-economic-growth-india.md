# Source notes — Economic Growth in India: Pattern and Structure

These notes are limited to dated, source-specific claims. They do not establish official MPPSC syllabus alignment.

## National accounts statistical base

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), in its 31 August 2026 PIB release for *National Accounts Statistics — 2026*, states that the publication is for the new national-accounts base year 2022–23. It includes updated Final Estimates for FY2022–23 and FY2023–24, First Revised Estimates for FY2024–25, and subsequent estimate vintages as described in the release. The new series replaces the earlier 2011–12 base-year series.

Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2304948&lang=1&reg=3

MoSPI's 21 September 2026 release on *Sources and Methods for Compilation of National Accounts Statistics* also describes the new 2022–23 base-year series and its update on 31 August 2026.
Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=295039

## Services sector: specify measure and period

The Government of India, *Economic Survey 2025–26*, Table I.2b and Chapter VII, reports the services share in **nominal GDP** as 53.6% in H1 FY2025–26 (versus 52.6% in H1 FY2024–25). The table gives 51.1% for FY2025–26 FAE. These are nominal-GDP shares and their stated periods; do not describe 53.6% as a full-year share or as the services share in GVA.

The Survey separately reports services GVA growth of 9.3% in H1 FY2025–26 and discusses the sector's role in GVA. When using a share statistic, identify whether it refers to GDP or GVA, nominal or real values, and H1 or full-year estimates.

Source: https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/economicsurvey/doc/echapter.pdf

## Employment structure: use a separate labour-force measure

The MoSPI *Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS), Annual Report 2025* reports that agriculture accounted for 43.0% of workers in usual status in 2025, down from 44.8% in 2024. The same PIB release notes manufacturing's employment share rose from 11.6% to 12.1% over that comparison. These are employment shares, not shares of GDP or GVA.

Source: Press Information Bureau, MoSPI, *PLFS Annual Report 2025*, released 27 March 2026: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009&lang=2&reg=48&regThe=

## Make in India

The Press Information Bureau states that Make in India was announced on 15 August 2014 and formally launched on 25 September 2014. Its stated objectives include facilitating investment, fostering innovation, building infrastructure, and positioning India as a hub for manufacturing, design and innovation.

Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2086347&lang=1&reg=3

## Editorial safeguards

- Do not mix GDP and GVA shares; identify metric, nominal/real basis, period and estimate vintage for every statistic.
- Use the 2022–23 base-year national-accounts series when citing the latest MoSPI national accounts; do not splice values from the old 2011–12 series into the new series without a clear bridge/statement.
- Do not present a one-period H1 service share as a timeless “service-led growth” fact or infer the employment structure from a GVA/GDP share.
- Do not repeat the retired/undated “25% manufacturing share target” as a current policy objective without a dated official source.
- Any illustrative sectoral table must be clearly marked hypothetical and must not masquerade as observed Indian national-accounts data.
- Clearly distinguish a sector's share in GDP from its contribution to GVA and distinguish nominal share from real growth.

## Human verification

Use the MoSPI data tables for exact sectoral values if the final lesson contains more detailed growth-series comparisons. The official releases and Survey are source leads, not a substitute for checking the final table, date labels, estimation vintage and bilingual interpretation. Human review remains mandatory before publication.
