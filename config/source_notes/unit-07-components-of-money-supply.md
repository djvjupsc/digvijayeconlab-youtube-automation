# Verified source notes — Components of Money Supply (India)

These notes are limited to historical definitions and working-group attribution. They do not claim that these are the only aggregates currently used for every RBI analytical purpose.

## Traditional monetary aggregates (M1–M4)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI), *Manual on Financial and Banking Statistics* (March 2007), Part I, Chapter I, Table 1.1, lists:
- M1 = Currency with the public + demand deposits with the banking system + other deposits with the RBI.
- M2 = M1 + savings deposits of post office savings banks.
- M3 = M1 + time deposits with the banking system.
- M4 = M3 + all deposits with post office savings banks, excluding National Savings Certificates.

The same RBI manual's methodological comparison identifies the Second Working Group (SWG) as 1977 and cites: *Report of the Second Working Group (Chairman M. L. Ghosh) on “Money Supply in India: Concepts, Compilation and Analysis”*, RBI, Bombay, 1977. Therefore, wording should identify this as the **Second Working Group on Money Supply (1977)**, not an unspecified working group.

## New monetary aggregates (NM1–NM3)

The RBI, *Comprehensive Guide for Current Statistics of the RBI Monthly Bulletin: Concepts, Definitions and Methodologies* (June 2017), states that the new monetary/banking aggregates are set out in the Report of the Working Group on Money Supply: Analytics and Methodology of Compilation (WGMS), chaired by Dr. Y. V. Reddy, June 1998. It distinguishes NM1, NM2 and NM3 from the existing aggregates.

The RBI guide and the RBI 2007 manual give these definitions:
- NM1 = Currency with the public + demand deposits with the banking system + other deposits with the RBI.
- NM2 = NM1 + short-term time deposits of residents (up to and including a contractual maturity of one year).
- NM3 = NM2 + long-term time deposits of residents + call/term funding from financial institutions.

The 1998 WGMS is distinct from the 1977 Second Working Group. Do not attribute NM1–NM3 to the 1977 group. Do not describe NM1–NM3 merely as “modern liquidity concepts”; describe them as the new monetary aggregates proposed by the 1998 WGMS.

## Editorial safeguards

- Preserve the distinction between M1–M4 (traditional aggregates) and NM1–NM3 (new aggregates proposed by the 1998 WGMS).
- Keep historical dates and committee attributions exactly as supported above.
- Do not call the 1998 NM aggregates a new or current RBI publication unless separately dated evidence is supplied.
- Any present-day policy or statistical usage claim beyond these historical definitions requires a separate, current RBI source.
