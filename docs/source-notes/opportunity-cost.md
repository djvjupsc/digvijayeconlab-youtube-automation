# Source notes — Opportunity Cost pilot

These notes are a limited reference set for the FreeLLMAPI one-lesson integration test. They are not a substitute for the official MPPSC syllabus or a complete textbook bibliography.

## Supported economic propositions

1. **Opportunity cost:** The value of the next-best alternative forgone when a choice is made.
   - Reference: Khan Academy, *Opportunity cost & the production possibilities curve (PPC)*.
   - https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/ap-macroeconomics/basic-economics-concepts-macro/production-possibilities-curve-scarcity-choice-and-opportunity-cost-macro/a/lesson-summary-opportunity-cost-and-the-ppc

2. **Production possibility frontier:** The PPF/PPC represents feasible production combinations given resources and technology. A bowed-out/concave-to-origin frontier is associated with increasing opportunity cost; a straight-line frontier represents constant opportunity cost under the model's assumptions.
   - Reference: Khan Academy, *Opportunity cost & the production possibilities curve (PPC)*.
   - https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/ap-macroeconomics/basic-economic-concepts-macro/production-possibilities-curve-scarcity-choice-and-opportunity-cost-macro/a/lesson-summary-opportunity-cost-and-the-ppc
   - Reference: SATHEE/IIT Kanpur, NCERT, *Introductory to Microeconomics, Chapter 1: Introduction*.
   - https://sathee.iitk.ac.in/sathee-ssc/student-corner/ncert-books/class-12/economics/introductory-to-microeconomics/chapter-01-introduction/

3. **Explicit and implicit costs:** Explicit costs are actual out-of-pocket payments. Implicit costs are opportunity costs of resources already owned and used by the firm. Accounting profit deducts explicit costs; economic profit deducts both explicit and implicit costs.
   - Reference: OpenStax, *Principles of Microeconomics 3e*, section 7.1.
   - https://openstax.org/books/principles-microeconomics-3e/pages/7-1-explicit-and-implicit-costs-and-accounting-and-economic-profit

## Numerical illustration controls

- Treat all rupee amounts and rates in generated questions as hypothetical unless a supplied source establishes otherwise.
- Forgone salary and forgone interest on owner-provided capital can be implicit opportunity costs when those alternatives are genuinely sacrificed.
- Purchased supplies and wages paid to hired workers are explicit costs.
- Do not double-count an amount. Clearly state whether a question asks for one category of cost or total economic cost.
